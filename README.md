## // WHO IS SAKSHAM?

I’m **Saksham Jagetiya**, a B.Tech Computer Science & Engineering student specializing in **AI & Analytics**.

I like building things that sit between **AI, software and real problems** — from machine learning and NLP systems to full-stack products, backend workflows and tools that question whether an AI system can actually be trusted.

I’m especially interested in the part after the demo: **What happens when the data is messy? When the model is wrong? When two users hit the same workflow at once? When an AI answer sounds confident but isn't reliable?**

That is the kind of engineering I’m trying to get better at.

### What I’m building toward

**AI / ML** — intelligent systems, NLP, prediction and model evaluation  
**AI Reliability** — hallucination analysis, failure modes, consistency and trust  
**Product Engineering** — full-stack apps, APIs, databases and real workflows  
**Data & Analytics** — turning raw data into useful decisions

I’m currently working as an **AI & Tech Intern** and building projects that push me beyond notebook-only ML. My recent work spans multimodal interview analysis, AI reliability, decision systems, credit risk, finance and full-stack applications.
